## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
The brand balances high-luxury agency craftsmanship with celestial futurism. It evokes mystery, architectural precision, infinite creative potential, and confidence. Interfaces convey the ambiance of deep space illuminated by distant nebula light, glowing starlight, and orbital geometry. The emotional response is intentional: quiet awe, sophisticated prestige, and uncompromising technical finesse.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Glassmorphism** and **Futuristic Minimalism**.
- **Dark Void Layers**: Deep obsidian canvases layered over subtly shifting cosmic violet atmospheres.
- **Glassmorphic Paneling**: Translucent card structures (`rgba(17, 17, 31, 0.65)`) with fine cosmic perimeter strokes, background blur, and inner specular top-edge highlights.
- **Orbital Accents**: Fine hair-thin rings, radial lavender glows, and starlight cross-flares that serve as structural embellishments without polluting core content readability.

## Colors

### Palette System
- **Canvas Base (`#050509`)**: Deepest Midnight Black void, establishing non-distracting background depth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0B0B16`)**: Deep Cosmos used for section grouping, sidebars, and structural wells.
- **Obsidian Container (`#11111F`)**: Semi-transparent card and container base, enhanced with backdrop blur.
- **Primary Accent (`#A970FF`)**: Cosmic Purple / Lavender Glow, utilized for interactive states, key CTAs, focal icons, and active indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B9C9FF`)**: Moon Blue / Starlight, deployed for secondary badges, subtle hover outlines, and orbital light rays.
- **Structural Stroke (`#29243A`)**: Subtle Cosmic Border for crisp separation without harsh visual weight.
- **Text & Content**: Pure Soft White (`#FFFFFF`) for display headlines and high-priority labels; Muted Stardust (`#B8B8C8`) for legible, non-fatiguing body text and metadata.

### Atmospheric Gradients
- `radial-cosmic`: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.18) 0%, rgba(5, 5, 9, 0) 70%)`
- `border-luminescence`: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.4) 0%, rgba(41, 36, 58, 0.4) 50%, rgba(185, 201, 255, 0.2) 100%)`

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Role Distribution
- **Headings & Display (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, futuristic, and structurally precise. High-impact statements leverage tighter letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) to maintain editorial density.
- **Controls, Navigation & Subheadings (`Manrope`)**: Geometric yet balanced and open, providing clarity for chips, pill links, navigation headers, and form inputs.
- **Body & Longform Reading (`Inter`)**: High-legibility neutral workhorse ensuring optical comfort over dark surfaces. Body text is assigned `#B8B8C8` to avoid the stark eye-strain of solid white.
- **Decorative Expressions**: When introducing script highlights or monumental classic touches, integrate handwritten accents or classical serif sub-titles sparingly for high-fashion editorial contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px max-width container, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, centered with 2rem (`32px`) baseline safe margin.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid container down to 768px, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, 1.5rem (`24px`) edge margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: 375px–767px, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1rem (`16px`) side margins. Stack grid columns vertically into single-column modules.

### Rhythm & Vertical Spacing
Major landing sections rely on breathing room (`space-xl` to `80px–120px` section gaps) to reflect the unhurried calm of space. Card internals use unified padding: `space-md` on compact panels and `space-lg` on feature panels.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Glows & Glassmorphic Stacking
Depth is achieved through luminance and atmospheric diffraction rather than opaque drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Void)**: `#050509`, base background plane.
- **Level 1 (Structural Sections)**: `#0B0B16` with ambient purple backlight `box-shadow: 0 0 120px -30px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Floating Modules)**: Background `#11111F` rendered at 70% opacity, combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and border `1px solid #29243A`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hover & Focus)**: Card lifts slightly (`translateY(-4px)`), border shifts toward `rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.5)`, casting an outer aura `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays)**: `#11111F` (90% opacity), `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, outline `1px solid rgba(185, 201, 255, 0.35)`, shadow `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(5, 5, 9, 0.8), 0 0 40px -4px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

### Geometry & Curvature
This design system embraces an aerodynamic, capsule-inspired shape language (`roundedness: 3`):
- **Buttons, Badges, Search Bars**: Full pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`) creating smooth orbital silhouettes.
- **Interactive Cards & Surface Enclosures**: Generous rounding (`rounded-lg: 2rem` / `rounded-xl: 3rem`) for a fluid, polished, and futuristic aesthetic.
- **Form Controls & Compact Tooltips**: Standard capsule or `1rem` soft-radius corners to preserve clear tap-target definitions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cosmic Aura Pill)**: Full pill silhouette. Filled with Cosmic Purple (`#A970FF`) to Moon Blue (`#B9C9FF`) gradient, or solid `#A970FF`. Text is pure `#FFFFFF` with `Manrope 600`. Outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.45)`. On hover, glow expands to 28px radius with a slight scale (`scale(1.02)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill**: Transparent background, border `1px solid #29243A`, text `#B9C9FF`. On hover, surface fills with `rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.08)` and border illuminates to `rgba(185, 201, 255, 0.6)`.

### Cards & Feature Panels
- Glass-backed obsidian panels (`rgba(17, 17, 31, 0.7)`), 16px blur filter, 1px border colored with `#29243A`.
- Feature cards contain an optional subtle linear gradient highlight on the top border (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(185, 201, 255, 0.3)`).
- Card icons sit inside floating circular or squircle pods (`rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.12)`) accented by glowing violet SVG strokes.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact pill height (32px), `padding: 0 14px`, `Manrope` 12px uppercase or semi-bold.
- **Default State**: Surface `rgba(17, 17, 31, 0.8)`, border `1px solid #29243A`, text `#B8B8C8`.
- **Selected State**: Background `linear-gradient(135deg, #A970FF, #7A3DFF)`, text `#FFFFFF`, shadow `0 0 14px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.4)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background `rgba(11, 11, 22, 0.8)`, border `1px solid #29243A`, border-radius `1rem`. Text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#5A566E`.
- **Focus State**: Border shifts to `#A970FF`, accompanied by an ambient ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 20px. Base border `#29243A` on `#0B0B16`.
- Checked state fills with `#A970FF`, displaying a crisp white micro checkmark or centered orbital dot. Active glow `0 0 10px rgba(169, 112, 255, 0.5)`.

### Specialty Elements (Orbital Dividers & Badges)
- Hairline horizontal dividers (`height: 1px`) featuring a central glow node: a linear fade from transparent to `#A970FF` at center, tapering off to transparent.
- Starlight flare micro-icons (`✦`) used as bullet decorators and section headline pre-tags.
